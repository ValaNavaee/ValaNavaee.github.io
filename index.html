About

Eight years of painting, aimed at places that feel lived-in and wrong.

Vala is an environment concept artist and visual development artist with eight years of painting experience and a background in game art and worldbuilding. He builds atmospheric environments through a 3D-assisted workflow across Blender, Unreal Engine and Unity, with work spanning games, board games and animation pre-production.

He's currently completing an MA in Game Design at Bahçeşehir University, where he also assists in teaching narrative design and psychology in games.
